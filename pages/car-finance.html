Eligibility & Required Documents

Prepare these standard documents to speed up your bank approval across Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad.

🪪

Salaried Individuals

Valid CNIC copy, 2 passport photos, latest 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statement, and employer verification letter. Minimum age 21 years.

💼

Business Owners & Self-Employed

Valid CNIC, NTN certificate & tax returns (last 2 years), business proof / letterhead, and 12 months active business bank statement.

🛡️

Mandatory Vehicle Checks

Used cars up to 9 years old require physical FastWheels 200+ Point Inspection, comprehensive insurance, and biometric excise transfer clearance.
